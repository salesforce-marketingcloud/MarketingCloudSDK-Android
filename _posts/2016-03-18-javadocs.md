### Salesforce Marketing Cloud Mobile Unified SDK

The Salesforce Marketing Cloud Mobile Unified SDK is a comprehensive solution that supports both the [MobilePush SDK](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/marketing/mobile-messaging-sdks/guide/mobilepush-sdk-intro.html) and [Mobile App Messaging SDK](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/marketing/mobile-messaging-sdks/guide/mam-sdk-intro.html). The SFMCSdk serves as the foundation for both products, enabling them to be used independently or together depending on your Marketing Cloud implementation.
